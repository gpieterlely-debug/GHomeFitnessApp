# Orthopedic visit — 19 Nov 2026 (one-page brief)

**Bring:** MRI report, this page, a list of current medications.
**Ask for:** your restrictions **in writing**. The app cannot unlock anything without a source document.

## 30-second summary to read out
1. Left rectus femoris: high-grade partial tendon tear, plus rectus femoris and iliopsoas muscle injury. Injured 22 Oct (4 weeks ago).
2. MRI: mild tendon laxity, no complete retraction.
3. Walking pain 2–3/10. Walking is tolerated. No physio yet.
4. Goals: cycling, running, kettlebell training, kicking a ball.

## Must-ask (top 5)
1. Exactly where is the tear, and how much of the tendon is involved?
2. **Does waiting change my surgical options or outcome?**
3. Am I cleared for gentle isometrics? Which exercises, at what effort, for how many sets and holds?
4. Am I cleared for a stationary bike? What seat height, resistance and duration?
5. Will you refer me to physio? When is my follow-up?

## Also ask
6. Weight-bearing instructions: do I need crutches?
7. What range of motion is allowed? What should I avoid (e.g., deep hip extension with a bent knee)?
8. Which supported upper-body work is OK? Are kettlebell swings, cleans and deadlifts off-limits?
9. Which symptoms mean I should stop and call you?
10. I will run my own functional tests. Which tests should a clinician do instead (strength and hop symmetry)?
11. Do I need repeat imaging? When?
